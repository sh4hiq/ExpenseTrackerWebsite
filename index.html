ExpenseTracker
Privacy Policy
Personal · Local-first

Understand your expenses, with less manual work.

ExpenseTracker is a personal application designed to help its owner organize expense information from email receipts and notifications.

Runs locally on the owner's computer

Gmail Integration

With the owner's explicit authorization, the application uses Google OAuth and the Gmail API to read email data needed for its expense-tracking workflow.

Personal Use

The application is intended for the owner's private use. It is not offered as a public service to other users.

Privacy

The application is designed to process data locally on the owner's computer, except for communications required by Google APIs or any additional services explicitly configured. See the privacy policy for details.
