Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Scope: ExpenseTracker is a personal application designed to run locally on its owner's computer. It is not intended to be a public service for other users.

1. Information Accessed

When the owner authorizes access, ExpenseTracker uses Google OAuth and the Gmail API to access email data within the permissions (OAuth scopes) configured for the application. Depending on those permissions and the application's functionality, this may include message content, sender information, subject lines, dates, and other email fields needed for expense tracking.

2. How Information Is Used

Email data is used for the owner's personal expense-tracking workflow, including identifying and organizing purchase receipts and expense-related notifications. The application is not designed to sell email data or use it for advertising.

3. Local Processing and Disclosure

The application is intended to process and store data locally on the owner's computer. Data is transmitted to Google when necessary for authentication and Gmail API requests.

If the owner configures additional integrations, cloud storage, synchronization, backups, or external services, data may also be transmitted to those services. Any such behavior must be reflected in this policy.

4. Storage and Security

Email data and OAuth credentials may be stored on the owner's computer as required by the application. The owner is responsible for protecting the computer and keeping credential files, access tokens, refresh tokens, and email data secure.

Credentials, tokens, and private email data should never be committed to a public source-code repository.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it or the application removes it according to its retention behavior. The owner can delete locally stored application data and revoke the application's Google access.

Revoking Google access does not automatically delete data that has already been stored locally.

6. Google Account Permissions

The owner can review or revoke the application's access through their Google Account permissions .

Revoking access prevents future API access unless authorization is granted again.

7. Sharing and Third Parties

ExpenseTracker is intended for personal use and is not designed to disclose Gmail data to other people. Google services are used for authentication and Gmail API access and are subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

If the application sends data to any other third-party service, this section must be updated to accurately describe that service and the data involved.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data handling changes. The date at the top should be updated whenever material changes are made.

9. Contact

For questions about this personal application or this policy, contact the application owner using the support email address listed in the Google Auth Platform Branding settings.
